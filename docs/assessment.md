# Assessment

| Criterion | Weight | Standard |
|---|---:|---|
| Diagnosis | 20 | Identifies the dominant mechanism and separates cause from symptom. |
| Quantitative evidence | 25 | Uses the correct measure, transparent assumptions, and interpreted results. |
| Stress and uncertainty | 20 | Challenges one material assumption and explains model limits. |
| Recommendation | 25 | Gives a feasible action and an observable or numerical reversal condition. |
| Communication | 10 | Produces a concise, board-ready artifact with effective visuals. |

!!! note "One integrated submission"
    Submit the decision artifact, notebook output, and source note as one coherent package. Do not submit disconnected answers to a question list.

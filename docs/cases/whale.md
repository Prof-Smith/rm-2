# London Whale

<div class="case-callout"><span>PRIMARY LENS</span><h2>Market risk measurement</h2><p>A concentrated credit portfolio produces losses beyond familiar VaR intuition.</p></div>

## Decision prompt
What should leadership do now, what evidence supports the action, and what result would reverse the recommendation?

## Evidence boundary
This case uses a concise historical frame and a **synthetic teaching dataset**. Treat the numbers as instructional inputs rather than audited historical facts.

## Analytical toolkit
VaR, Expected Shortfall, backtesting, limit governance.

## Investigation
1. Identify the dominant failure mechanism.
2. Separate warning indicators from outcome measures.
3. Calculate the module's core risk measure.
4. Stress one assumption that management is likely to underestimate.
5. Recommend one action and define a reversal condition.

## Deliverable
Produce the **Market Risk Brief** using the common decision standard. Keep the main narrative concise and move calculations, charts, and source details to exhibits.

# Course map

<div class="lead">Eight modules build one cumulative capability: making defensible decisions when models, markets, incentives, and institutions interact.</div>

|---|---|---|---|

| 3. Model Risk and Leverage | What happens when a coherent model meets a changed regime? | LTCM | Model Challenge Memo |
| 4. Liquidity and Funding | Can the institution survive long enough for solvency to matter? | Lehman Brothers | Liquidity Survival Plan |
| 5. Credit and Counterparty Risk | How does deterioration migrate across exposures? | Credit Portfolio | Counterparty Risk Assessment |
| 6. Systemic Risk and Contagion | How does a local shock become a system event? | Global Financial Crisis | Systemic Risk Map |
| 7. Governance and Regulation | Which controls change behavior before loss becomes irreversible? | Atlas Bank International | Board Controls Memo |
| 8. Enterprise Risk and the CRO Challenge | Which risks matter most when they interact? | Sunrise National Bank | CRO Board Pack |
